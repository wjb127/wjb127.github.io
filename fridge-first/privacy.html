Fridge First privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026. Publisher WJB Apps. Support: wjb127@naver.com.

Food inventory, quantities, locations, entered package dates, storage overrides, consumption/discard status and reminder settings stay on your device. The publisher receives none of this inventory. No account, camera, barcode scanning, location permission or health access is required. Uninstalling removes local app data; device backups follow your operating system settings.

Optional local notifications require permission. Reminders use device local time and a 14-day schedule refreshed when you open or update the app. Your OS may delay delivery. Denying notifications preserves all inventory functions.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP-derived coarse location, advertising interactions and diagnostics to provide and measure ads. Regional Google User Messaging Platform consent is checked before eligible ad requests. Advertising privacy choices are available when required. Refusal, offline use and unavailable advertising preserve inventory functions. Google privacy policy.

Storage guidance is based on FoodKeeper data and helps plan freshness and quality. It does not establish food safety or replace manufacturer instructions. Use the condition matching your actual food, storage and purchase/opening/thawing date. Printed dates cap suggested management dates. Official FoodKeeper source.
