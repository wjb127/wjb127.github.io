Your score sheets

Player nicknames, scores, settings, unfinished games and up to 20 finished games are stored on your device. We operate no account or server for these records and do not receive them. Copying a result places the selected text on your device clipboard. Use nicknames rather than private information.

Advertising and consent

The Android and iOS apps use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Banner ads appear on the setup and history screen, never over the active score board. Depending on platform, consent, permissions and SDK configuration, Google may collect IP address (which can estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions and diagnostic/performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain services. We do not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Regional consent messages are shown where required. You can revisit applicable choices from “Ad privacy choices” in the app menu. On iOS, tracking across other companies' apps or websites requires Apple's permission when requested. Refusing tracking or ads consent does not prevent score keeping; ads may be limited or unavailable. Learn more in Google's privacy policy and Google advertising information.

Storage, removal and transfers

Your score sheets stay locally unless you choose to copy and share them, or your operating system includes them in its device backup. Removing the app and its local data removes these records; system backups are controlled by your operating system settings. Google handles advertising data under its own retention policies and may process it in other countries. Contact Google for advertising-data rights, and contact us for questions about this app.

Audience and updates

This general-purpose game-night utility is not designed or marketed specifically to children. We do not knowingly receive children's personal information. This policy will be updated when app behavior or service providers change.
